What it does

One system, the whole fees office.

Built to replace the old Access database schools were patching for years — with real dates, enforced links between tables, and nothing that can be quietly edited or deleted.

Students & enrolment

Register a whole school at once from Excel sheets teachers fill in by hand, or set up every class in one upload instead of typing each one. Promote a whole school up a grade at year end in a few clicks.

new: bulk class setup

Fees & invoicing

Bill each term or each month. Take USD, ZiG or any currency the school needs, kept strictly separate or converted at the till with a logged exchange rate — your choice.

Payments & receipts

Numbered receipt books (one for everything, or one per payment method), a live Debtors report, and instalment schedules worked out automatically from the term's own dates.

Fund accounting

Every fee item is its own fund: what came in, less what was spent, down to the cent. Spending can never go past what a fund actually holds — the system simply refuses it.

Multi-PC, no internet

One PC in the office runs it; other PCs use it over the school's own network, even with nothing but a cheap router. Away from school with internet, Tailscale adds secure remote access.

Accountability built in

Nothing is ever silently edited or deleted. A payment can only be voided with a reason, by a named user, and it's all in the Activity log — sign-ins, failed sign-ins, voids, month-end, every user change.

Reports, every format

Debtors, payments, invoices, cashbook, funds, class lists — each one as PDF, Word, Excel or CSV, produced on the school's own PC. Nothing is sent to an online service.

Automatic backups

Backed up daily, on every sign-out, and on demand. Point it at a cloud-sync folder for an off-site copy, or e-mail an AES-256 password-protected copy to up to five addresses.

Offline by design

Your data stays at the school.

No internet, no problemNothing is loaded from the internet to run. The only part that ever needs a connection is optionally e-mailing a backup copy.
Hashed, logged, lockedPasswords are hashed, five wrong attempts lock an account for 15 minutes, and every sign-in — successful or not — is on record.
Encrypted off-site copiesAn e-mailed backup is an AES-256 password-protected zip. Without that password, nobody — including us — can open it.
Private by defaultThe local-network firewall rule never reaches outside the building. Remote access, if you want it, goes through your own private Tailscale network — never an open port.
